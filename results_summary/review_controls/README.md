# Review controls

Differences and intervals in `factorial_blocks_pp.csv`, `factorial_variation.csv` and the Dice contrasts in `capacity_contrasts.csv` are percentage points. Absolute Dice in `capacity_scores.csv` is on a 0–1 scale.

- **Stability.** The original seed-11 four-arm block and two independent complete refits share the patient split, training initialisation within arm, 40,000-update schedule, sampled crops, augmentation sequence and checkpoint selection rule. Row intervals in `factorial_blocks_pp.csv` resample the 40 paired patients. The Student t interval in `factorial_variation.csv` describes variation across the three fits conditional on this seed and these patients. In `factorial_six_seed_sensitivity_pp.csv`, each new seed-11 block replaces the original in the six-seed estimate; intervals resample patients and seeds.
  The secondary table `factorial_fixed_step_pp.csv` compares the final 40,000-update checkpoints with the development-selected checkpoint in each block.
- **Channel order.** Swap the two outer channels during inference in both frozen mirror layouts and training regimes, for seeds 44, 55 and 66. All comparisons use the same 40 replication patients and the threefold degraded input. Positive `fp_delta_ml` and `fn_delta_ml` indicate additional false-positive or false-negative volume after swapping. Native label disagreement uses all voxels in the native grid. The ordinary and swapped predictions are recomputed together. Drift of ordinary predictions from the archive is recorded, and archived Dice is checked against the original evaluation.
- **Capacity.** Compare 3D width 9 with planar width 16 and the original 3D width 16, using standard inputs, three seeds, 20,000 updates and the original 40-patient cohort. Lower HD95 indicates closer boundaries. The paired intervals resample patients after averaging training seeds.
- **Cost.** The new timer spans a preprocessed CT already in GPU memory to probabilities on the model grid. Whole-case timings from the original experiment include input resampling, transfers and native-grid restoration. The two timings were recorded separately. Training times are elapsed times from the nine original architecture fits.
